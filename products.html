01 / WHY BUSINESS OS[ 4 REASONS ]

Fewer apps, that work together.

Businesses don’t need more apps. They need fewer, that work together. Use the apps you need, switch more on as you grow, and everything connects automatically.

  1. 01

    One login, every app

    Your team signs in once and sees exactly the apps and features they’re allowed to use, in the same place, with the same look and feel.

  2. 02

    One source of truth

    A customer in the CRM is the same customer on the invoice, the quotation, the contract, the support ticket and the timesheet. Nothing is copied, nothing drifts.

  3. 03

    Apps that talk to each other

    Unpaid leave flows into payroll. An accepted quote becomes an invoice in one click. A hired candidate becomes an employee. A vendor bill becomes an expense.

  4. 04

    Built for Indian businesses

    GSTIN, PAN and IFSC validation, rupee formatting, Indian date formats, Indian holidays, loss-of-pay handling and more.

More included with every workspace: a bundle of DotN products, free, so your team gets more from day one.

02 / AI-POWERED[ 6 FEATURES ]

An operating system that understands your business.

Because every app shares the same information, Business OS gives AI the full picture of customers, money, people, projects and tickets together, while respecting exactly who is allowed to see what.

  1. 01

    Smart summaries

    One-paragraph summaries of long support tickets, candidate profiles, contract terms and project activity, so managers catch up in seconds.

  2. 02

    Auto-sort and route

    Incoming support tickets and internal requests are categorised, prioritised and sent to the right team automatically.

  3. 03

    Reads documents for you

    Pulls the important details out of resumes, vendor bills and contracts (names, amounts, dates, renewal terms) instead of typing them in.

  4. 04

    Search by meaning

    Find the right article, ticket or document even when you don’t remember the exact words: a company memory across your knowledge base, helpdesk and records.

  5. 05

    Writing help

    Drafts customer replies, job descriptions, follow-up emails and campaign copy in your company’s tone, ready for you to review and send.

  6. 06

    Forecasts and early warnings

    Cash-flow forecasts from invoices, payroll and pipeline; which deals are likely to close; which customers may be at risk, with the reasons shown, not just a score.

Private by design: your business information stays inside Business OS. Companies that already use an AI service can connect their own account instead. Every AI feature follows the same permissions as the rest of the system.
