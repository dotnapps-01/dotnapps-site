About

We build software for other companies — and our own.

Dotnapps is a studio and a product company in one. We design and ship digital products for clients, and we build and run Business OS, our own suite of business apps. The same team does both — that’s the whole point.

How it fits together

A studio and a product company, run by one team.

Dotnapps is two things at once. We design and build software for other companies, and we build and run Business OS, our own suite of business apps. Neither is a side project.

The two halves feed each other. Client work brings us hard problems from a dozen industries at once. Business OS gives us a product we own, operate, and support at scale — with real customers and real uptime. The same engineers and designers move between both.

It also keeps us honest. Every data model, billing flow, and onboarding we ship for a client is the kind we’d be willing to run ourselves for years — because on Business OS, we do.

Two sides, one team

How we work

Principles

Six things we don’t compromise on.

Own the outcome

We judge a project by what it does in production, not by what we handed over.

One team, both sides

The engineers on your project also build and run Business OS. There is no junior bench.

Senior by default

Every project runs on people who have shipped before. Fewer handoffs, less translation loss.

Boring tech on purpose

TypeScript, React, Postgres, one cloud. Predictable beats novel when someone has to maintain it.

Design is a discipline

One monochrome system, no decorative noise. Constraints keep the product legible.

Ship weekly

Something real in front of you every week. Momentum is a feature.
