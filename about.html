About

We build software for other companies — and our own.

Dotnapps is a product and engineering studio. We design and ship digital products for clients, and we run Business OS, our own suite of business apps. The same people work on both — that’s the whole point.

  • Founded 2026
  • Product & engineering studio

  • Remote, senior team
Why we exist

Teams for hire rarely live with what they ship.

Dotnapps started with a simple frustration: most software teams for hire never have to maintain what they build. The engagement ends, the invoice clears, and the codebase becomes someone else’s problem.

So we build our own products too. Business OS is a growing suite of business applications — CRM and Invoice are live, with a dozen more mapped — that we own, run, and sell. Real customers, real uptime, a real support queue. It keeps us honest.

Client work is held to that same bar. When we design a data model, a billing flow, or an onboarding, it’s the kind we’d be willing to run ourselves for years — because on Business OS, we do.

How we work

Principles

Six things we don’t compromise on.

Own the outcome

We judge a project by what it does in production, not by what we handed over.

One team, both sides

The engineers on your project also build and run Business OS. There is no junior bench.

Small and senior

Tight teams of people who have shipped before. Fewer handoffs, less translation loss.

Boring tech on purpose

TypeScript, React, Postgres, one cloud. Predictable beats novel when someone has to maintain it.

Design is a discipline

One monochrome system, no decorative noise. Constraints keep the product legible.

Ship weekly

Something real in front of you every week. Momentum is a feature.
