Our idea

The turban is a promise.

In Indian tradition, a turban crowns the person being honoured. That is how we plan: the host is the guest of honour, every guest is welcomed like family, and every detail — from the first invitation to the last farewell — carries the same respect.

Calm

A control room that absorbs the chaos so the family never feels it.

        Good questions

        Before you ask.

        How early should we book?

        For weddings we suggest four to six months; for corporate and family events, four to eight weeks. Shorter notice? Ask anyway — we’ll tell you honestly what’s possible.

        Can you arrange traditional entries — horse chariot, palki, nadaswaram?

        Yes. We coordinate the whole entry: the chariot or palki, musicians and melam, pyro, and the route, timing and safety planning that make it smooth.

        Can we use our own venue, caterer or photographer?

        Of course. We’re happy to fold your favourite vendors into the plan, or recommend trusted ones where you need them.

        Do you travel outside Tamil Nadu?

        We plan events across Tamil Nadu and travel anywhere in India for destination weddings, retreats and multi-day celebrations.

        How does pricing work?

        The estimator above gives an early planning range. After a short conversation we send a written quote with every line itemised — no surprises on the day.

        What if our plans change?

        They often do. We build flexibility into timelines and vendor terms from the start, and we tell you early about anything that affects the day.
