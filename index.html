SEC. 12 / FAQ[ 4 TOPICS ]

We have the answers

Straight answers on how we scope, price, work, and hand over.

Ask a question

Scoping·Pricing·Working·Handover

FIG. 12 / STRAIGHT ANSWERS
What does Dotnapps do?

We design and build software for other companies, and we run our own product line, Business OS. A studio and a product company in one.

Products or services — which is it?

Both. Services are how we help your team ship; Business OS is software we own and sell. The same people work on both.

Do you work remotely?

Yes. We work with teams across time zones, with overlap hours agreed up front.

How long does an MVP take?

Usually 6–12 weeks to something real in users' hands, depending on scope and how much of it is genuinely new.

How do you price work?

Fixed price for well-defined scope, a monthly rate for a dedicated team, a day rate for advisory. We scope before we quote.

Is there a minimum engagement?

Advisory starts at a few days. Build work is typically four weeks or more — below that, a freelancer is usually the better fit.

Do you work with our existing team?

Yes — we embed, pair, and hand over. Advisory engagements assume you already have engineers.

What's the process?

Discovery, design, build in weekly slices, then scale. You see working software every week, not one big reveal.

What happens after launch?

We stay for a support window, then move to maintenance or a dedicated-team arrangement if you want it.

Who owns the code and IP?

You do, from day one. Repositories, infrastructure, and accounts are yours throughout.

Do you sign NDAs?

Yes, before the first detailed conversation if you need it.

How do you handle our data and security?

Least-access by default, your cloud or ours, and we follow your compliance requirements — we build regulated products already.
